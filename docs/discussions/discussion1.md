# Travel itinerary planning ideas

Fri, 18 Sept 26

#### Travel Platform Concept

- Two-sided marketplace for experiential travel, focused on authentic local experiences
- Demand side: travelers co-create itineraries with friends/family
  - Drag-and-drop timeline with start/end dates
  - Each person adds preferred experiences as blocks
  - Group voting/polling to lock in activities
  - Travel fund with debit order contributions for upfront cost relief
- Supply side: local entrepreneurs list experiences (cooking classes, guided walks, craft markets, suit tailoring, etc.)
  - Core problem: Airbnb Experiences excludes low-tech local suppliers
  - Solution: WhatsApp-based onboarding for suppliers with no internet/tech access
  - AI middle layer compiles listing, writes description, recommends pricing, generates images
  - Booking confirmations and payments flow back through WhatsApp
- Filtering for travelers: cost, impact, sustainability, local enrichment, renewable energy, aesthetics
  - Incentivize suppliers to be “greener” with higher placement/visibility in algorithm
- Self-guided tour mode: GPS-triggered audio commentary at checkpoints, in traveler’s own language
- Offline mode flagged as essential: itinerary, translator, photo storage (connectivity unreliable abroad)

#### Supply-Side Sourcing and Safety

- Sourcing low-tech suppliers: human outreach required, can’t rely on self-registration
  - Reference model: Yazzie (WhatsApp-based surveys across Africa, pays 10 ZAR per response)
  - Idea: crowdsource supplier recommendations via WhatsApp surveys (“who runs the best tour in your area?”)
  - Craft market parallel: photograph and list artifacts on behalf of vendors who won’t self-list
- WhatsApp bot guides suppliers to load their experience
  - Prompts: name, offering, photos, price, sustainability attributes
  - Data pushed to front-end platform; supplier never needs a website
- Safety and trust mechanisms discussed:
  - Identity verification (ID number)
  - Human vetting: dummy experience runs, checking equipment and routes
  - Optional security detail add-on at booking (partner with security companies)
  - Insurance as a modular bolt-on
  - Transport as another potential add-on

#### Problem Statement and Impact Angle

- Core problem: how to create authentic experiential travel that enriches local communities
- Financial inclusion: travel fund/savings model for less wealthy travelers
- Tech inclusion: WhatsApp-first supply side removes barriers for local entrepreneurs
- Impact is the key differentiator and judging criterion
  - Goal: empower local South Africans to participate in and profit from the tourism economy
- Memorable travel insight from user interviews (15 people): the most memorable moments are unscripted
  - Example: 70,000 ZAR/night Kruger lodge guest said the lioness-and-cub encounter beat everything else
  - Implication: platform should support capture and sharing of spontaneous moments, not just planned itineraries
